WHAT GETS ENGINEERED

Voice AI is a real-time product system, not a talking model.

A production voice product has to manage speech, timing, context, workflow state, system access, permissions, failures, and human handoff while the conversation is happening. Each layer affects whether the experience feels natural—and whether the system can be trusted to act.

01CONVERSATION & TURN BEHAVIOR

Make the interaction feel natural before making it intelligent.

Design how the system opens, listens, pauses, interrupts, handles silence, repairs misunderstandings, asks clarifying questions, and knows when the user has finished speaking.

Engineering areasTurn detectionBarge-inSilence handlingInterruptionsRepair loopsConversational pacing

Keep the conversation responsive under real conditions.

Engineer the real-time speech path across phone, web, or application channels—including recognition or speech-to-speech processing, synthesis, streaming, audio quality, connection state, and latency.

Engineering areasStreaming audioSpeech recognitionSynthesisRealtime modelsTelephony / web channelsLatencyNoise handling
03INTENT, CONTEXT & CONVERSATION STATE

Understand more than the last sentence.

Maintain the user’s intent, entities, prior turns, workflow state, relevant business context, and what has already been confirmed so the system can reason across the full conversation.

Engineering areasIntentEntitiesSession stateContext retrievalMemoryWorkflow stateClarification
04TOOLS, SYSTEMS & WORKFLOW ACTION

Connect conversation to real work.

Define how the voice system reads information, calls APIs, searches systems, updates records, schedules activity, triggers workflows, and coordinates multiple tools safely.

Engineering areasTool schemasAPIsCRM / scheduling / business systemsOrchestrationRetriesAction state
05PERMISSIONS, CONFIRMATION & GUARDRAILS

The ability to act does not mean permission to act.

Determine who the user is, what the system may access, what actions require explicit confirmation, what is restricted, and what must be escalated to a person.

Engineering areasAuthenticationAuthorizationPermission checksConfirmationsRestricted actionsAudit context
06RECOVERY, ESCALATION & HUMAN HANDOFF

Design failure as part of the product.

When intent is unclear, a system is unavailable, an action fails, confidence is insufficient, or human judgment is required, the voice experience should recover gracefully and transfer useful context rather than simply stopping.

Engineering areasFallbacksRetry strategyException handlingConfidence thresholdsEscalationContext-rich handoff
07EVALUATION & PRODUCTION OBSERVABILITY

Know what is happening inside every conversation.

Measure both conversational quality and operational outcomes: interruptions, latency, misunderstood intent, tool failures, abandoned tasks, escalation quality, completion, and user correction.
