WHAT GETS ENGINEERED

An intelligent product is a system of connected layers.

A useful AI product requires more than a model endpoint. Product decisions, user experience, data, application logic, models, integrations, evaluation, controls, and production operations have to work together as one system.

Decide what intelligence should—and should not—do.

Define the user, workflow, business outcome, constraints, and success criteria. Identify where AI creates meaningful value and where deterministic logic, rules, or human judgment should remain in control.

Design for intelligence, uncertainty, and human judgment.

AI changes how a product behaves. The experience has to make recommendations, generated outputs, uncertainty, confirmations, corrections, approvals, and escalation understandable to the person using it.
