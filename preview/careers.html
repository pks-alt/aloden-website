WHY ALODEN

More ownership. More context. Less distance from the outcome.

We are building Aloden around small, capable teams that can move from an important problem to working software without separating product judgment from technical execution.

01OWN THE OUTCOME

02WORK ACROSS DISCIPLINES

THE WORK

Build at the edge of what software is becoming.

Aloden works where product engineering, AI, modernization, and real operating workflows meet. The technology changes quickly; the standard for useful software does not.

01New intelligent products

HOW WE SHOW UP

The behaviors matter as much as the tools.

We value people who can think clearly, make progress visible, challenge assumptions respectfully, and take responsibility for the quality of what reaches users.

01Be curious

Ask enough questions to understand the real problem instead of solving the first version of it.

02Be clear

Explain decisions, uncertainty, tradeoffs, and risks so the team can move with shared context.

03Care about craft

Good work is thoughtful in the details—from code and architecture to interaction, documentation, and handoff.

04Take responsibility

Own the result, surface problems early, and stay engaged until the product behaves the way it should.

HOW WE HIRE

A practical process designed to understand how you work.

The exact process can vary by role, but the goal stays the same: give both sides enough signal to make a thoughtful decision without unnecessary ceremony.

01Apply or introduce yourself

02Conversation

Talk about the role, the work, what you want to learn, and what Aloden needs.

03Role / technical discussion

Go deeper on judgment, fundamentals, decisions, collaboration, and relevant work.

Align on fit, expectations, role details, and the path forward.

For some technical or early-career roles, a practical build, portfolio walkthrough, or hackathon output may provide better signal than a traditional interview exercise.

BEFORE YOU REACH OUT

A few practical answers.

How does résumé upload work?

Select one PDF or DOCX up to 5 MB, complete the required fields, and submit when online applications are enabled. A confirmation appears only after the server confirms acceptance. On a review link, file selection stays in your browser and nothing is sent. You can always email HR instead.

Are the talent areas current job openings?

The talent areas describe our work. Only the roles listed under Current Openings are published vacancies. Select View role and Apply for this role to attach that job reference to your application. You can also submit a separate general introduction.

Can students and recent graduates get in touch?

Yes. Ask about early-career or university opportunities and share a course project, personal build, or hackathon contribution. HR will confirm any available program, its dates, eligibility, and selection steps. Participation does not guarantee employment.

Are roles remote, hybrid, or office-based?

Working arrangements, eligible locations, employment type, compensation, and benefits depend on the specific opportunity. Ask HR for the details of the role you are discussing; this page does not promise a company-wide arrangement.

Can I share work made with AI tools?

Explain your contribution, which tools you used, and how you checked the result. Be ready to discuss the decisions and code. Follow the instructions given for any assessment; ask the hiring team when the permitted use of tools is unclear.

What should I leave out of an introduction?

Do not send government ID numbers, banking details, medical records, passwords, or confidential client code. Use a non-confidential project summary when you cannot share the original work. For website information, read our privacy notice.

How do I ask a question or discuss an accessibility need?

Email hr@aloden.com and describe the help you need; do not include medical documentation in an initial email. You can also use this address to verify an unexpected recruiting message before sharing personal information.
