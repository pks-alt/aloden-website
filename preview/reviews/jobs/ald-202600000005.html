Key responsibilities

  • Turn the three defined product journeys into clear test scenarios and acceptance checks.
  • Test the full flow across screens, roles, permissions, and backend updates.
  • Cover validation failures, interrupted steps, retries, empty states, and recovery behavior.
  • Check deadline boundaries, time-zone assumptions, and late or duplicate actions.
  • Report reproducible defects with steps, expected behavior, and useful evidence.
  • Retest fixes and communicate the remaining release risks to the team.

What you bring

  • Experience testing complete web-application journeys and writing reproducible defect reports.
  • Ability to reason about permissions, state changes, and cross-role interactions.
  • A systematic approach to failure paths and boundary conditions.
  • Clear written communication and attention to detail.
  • Ability to collaborate with engineers on acceptance criteria and retesting.

Helpful experience

  • Experience with browser test automation or API testing.
  • Experience testing time-sensitive workflows and multi-user platforms.
  • Examples of test plans or release-quality improvements you have contributed.
