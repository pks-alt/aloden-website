Key responsibilities

  • Implement product screens and reusable components from the approved wireframes.
  • Build forms with clear validation, accessible labels, and useful success and error states.
  • Represent role and visibility rules accurately while coordinating server-side enforcement with backend engineers.
  • Handle loading, empty, permission-denied, and failure states across the main journeys.
  • Check mobile, keyboard, and cross-browser behavior before release.
  • Collaborate on API integration, interaction details, and regression testing.

What you bring

  • Practical experience building component-based web interfaces and integrating APIs.
  • Strong HTML, CSS, JavaScript or TypeScript, and responsive-layout fundamentals.
  • Experience implementing accessible forms and stateful product interactions.
  • Ability to translate wireframes into consistent, usable screens.
  • A careful approach to testing permissions, edge cases, and error handling.

Helpful experience

  • Experience with React or Next.js product interfaces.
  • Experience with component libraries and automated browser tests.
  • Examples showing your contribution from design interpretation through production.
