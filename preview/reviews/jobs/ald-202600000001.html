Key responsibilities

  • Own technical architecture and translate product requirements into an implementable delivery plan.
  • Build and review Next.js application code, backend interfaces, and PostgreSQL integration.
  • Design role-based access controls and enforce permissions on the server, not only in the interface.
  • Coordinate frontend, backend, security, and QA work around shared interfaces and release criteria.
  • Prepare staging and production deployment, including the Hostinger deployment path identified in the project brief.
  • Document key decisions, review critical changes, and resolve risks before release.

What you bring

  • Hands-on full-stack delivery experience with Next.js and relational databases.
  • Ability to design application architecture, API boundaries, and role-based permissions.
  • Experience taking software from development into a monitored production environment.
  • Clear technical communication and practical code-review skills.
  • Ability to explain tradeoffs and take ownership across disciplines.

Helpful experience

  • Experience evolving existing codebases and delivering multi-user platforms.
  • Experience with automated testing, deployment pipelines, and recovery planning.
  • Examples of architectural decisions and production launches you have personally led.
