About this notice

Aloden LLC provides AI product engineering and digital modernization services. This privacy notice applies to the public Aloden website and to information you send through a project inquiry or by contacting us through the website.

Project inquiries are not a secure channel for sensitive information. Please do not submit confidential, proprietary, regulated, health, financial, credential, password, or other sensitive information in the initial project brief. We can establish an appropriate process for detailed information later.

Information we collect

Information you provide

If you submit a project brief, the information may include the project category, current product stage, areas where you want help, desired outcome, timing, company or organization, your name and work email, and optional information such as constraints, existing systems or data environment, and role or title.

If you contact us directly by email, we receive the information contained in your message and the contact information you use.

Information generated by use of the site

Our hosting, security, and delivery providers may process technical information needed to operate and protect the site, such as IP address, browser or device information, request timing, security signals, and server logs. When anti-abuse tools such as Cloudflare Turnstile are enabled, those tools may process information needed to distinguish legitimate users from automated abuse.

Analytics and similar technologies

We may use limited website analytics to understand site performance and usage. If our use of analytics or similar technologies materially changes, we will update this notice and any consent mechanism required by applicable law.

How we use information

We use information collected through the site to respond to project inquiries, understand the product or engineering need you described, communicate with you, operate and secure the website, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, improve the website and inquiry experience, and meet applicable legal or compliance obligations.

Submitting a project brief asks Aloden to contact you about that project. It does not by itself enroll you in unrelated marketing communications.

How information is shared

We may provide information to service providers that help us host, secure, operate, monitor, or deliver communications for the website, subject to appropriate service arrangements. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or security, or in connection with a corporate transaction where permitted by law.

We do not treat a project inquiry as permission to sell the information you submit. We do not disclose project-brief content to advertisers for their independent advertising use.

The site may link to third-party services, such as LinkedIn. Their privacy practices are governed by their own notices.

Security and retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the website and information being handled. No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so the initial project brief is intentionally designed to avoid requesting sensitive project information.

We retain inquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate business needs, security, recordkeeping, dispute resolution, or legal obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the interaction and the systems used to handle it.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to submit the project form and contact us directly by email instead. Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or other handling of personal information.

To make a privacy request, email hello@aloden.com. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

Children

The Aloden website and project inquiry experience are intended for business and professional audiences and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly design the project brief to collect information from children.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the website, project inquiry workflow, service providers, or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of the page will be updated when a revised notice is published.
