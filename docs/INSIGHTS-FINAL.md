## Editorial Lens

Headline: **New technology matters when it improves the product system.**

Decision framework:

1. **Product Value** — What becomes meaningfully better for the user, workflow, operation, or business?
2. **System Fit** — Where does the capability belong in architecture, data, workflow state, integrations, permissions, and product boundaries?
3. **Production Reality** — What changes when latency, reliability, security, privacy, cost, exceptions, human control, observability, and support become real requirements?
4. **Evidence** — How will we know the product actually improved?

Locked principle: **NOVELTY IS NOT THE OUTCOME**

Supporting statement: `We do not ask, “How do we add this technology?” We ask, “What problem earns its place in the product?”`

Closing principle: **The strongest technology decision is often knowing where AI should be used, where conventional software is better, and where people should remain in control.**

## From Insight to Product

Headline: **Good thinking should change what gets built.**

Decision-to-delivery flow:

`Signal / Question → Hypothesis → Product Decision → Controlled Test → Production Change → Evidence → Next Iteration`

Approved translations:

- **AI Product Engineering:** `Can the model do it?` → `Can the product system depend on it?`
- **Agentic Systems:** `Can the agent take action?` → `What may it act on, how is the result verified, and when should a person take over?`
- **Product Modernization:** `Can we replace the old system?` → `Which constraint is actually worth changing—and what value should be preserved?`
- **Healthcare AI:** `Can AI make this decision?` → `What context, evidence, permissions, and human accountability must remain around it?`

Closing: **Insights become valuable when they reduce uncertainty around the next product decision.**
