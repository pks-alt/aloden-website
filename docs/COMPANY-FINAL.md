## Locked positioning

Aloden is positioned as an **AI product engineering and digital modernization company**. The Company page must not drift back to generic IT services, staffing, or undifferentiated development-shop positioning.

Core idea: Aloden works from the product problem outward and keeps product strategy, experience design, AI, software engineering, modernization, integration, and production responsibility connected.

## Approved page structure

1. **Company Hero** — We build products around the reality they have to work in.
2. **Why Aloden** — The product should not be handed from discipline to discipline.
3. **How Aloden Works** — Discover → Design → Build → Deploy → Evolve; structured but not waterfall.
4. **What Makes Aloden Different** — Product ownership, AI engineering depth, modernization without rewrite reflex, real workflow understanding, and production accountability.
5. **Built as Proof** — Medlivo, StartupFair, and Aloden Voice AI as proof of one engineering discipline.
6. **Trust & Responsible Delivery** — Confidentiality, controlled delivery, quality and evaluation, responsible AI, security, reliability, accessibility, performance, observability, and accountability.
7. **Final CTA** — Start with the product.

## Locked Company principles

- Start with the problem.
- Design the product and system together.
- AI belongs inside the product architecture.
- Production is part of the design.
- Evolve with evidence.
- The product—not the department, technology, or project phase—is the unit of work.
- Modernization preserves what creates value and changes what materially constrains the product.
- More AI autonomy requires more explicit controls, verification, and accountability.
