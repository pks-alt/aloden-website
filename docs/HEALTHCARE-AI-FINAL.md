## Positioning

Healthcare AI is presented as a broad healthcare product and operating-system capability, not a therapy-only or staffing-only page. The page covers workforce, care/referrals, credentialing/readiness, scheduling/capacity, documentation/evidence, financial/operational workflows, connected systems, and human accountability.

AI is intentionally embedded inside workflow state, trusted healthcare context, rules, permissions, systems, verification, and human judgment rather than presented as a standalone chatbot or matching feature.

## Approved sections

1. **Hero — Healthcare AI**
   - Headline: `Build intelligence into the healthcare workflow—not beside it.`
   - Support: `Workforce. Care. Operations. Connected by AI.`
   - Scope includes Hospitals & Health Systems, Nursing & Allied, Physicians & APPs, Rehab, Home Health, and Workforce & Care Operations.
   - Healthcare Intelligence Core and broader platform visual retained.

2. **Healthcare Intelligence Architecture**
   - Headline: `AI becomes useful when it can see the workflow, the context, and the consequences.`
   - Layers: Healthcare Experience & Workflow; Product State & Orchestration; Healthcare Intelligence Core; Trusted Healthcare Context; Connected Healthcare Systems; Trust, Safety & Human Control.
   - AI loop: `Observe → Understand → Decide → Act → Verify → Learn`.
   - Authority boundary retained.

3. **Intelligence in the Workflow**
   - Headline: `One intelligence layer. Different healthcare journeys.`
   - Six journeys: Workforce & Facility Demand; Patient Referral & Home Health; Credentialing & Readiness; Scheduling, Capacity & Operations; Documentation & Evidence; Financial & Operational Intelligence.
   - Shared decision loop: `Context → AI Assistance → Rules / Policy → Human Decision → System Action → Verification → Updated State`.

4. **AI, Rules & Human Judgment**
   - Headline: `Put intelligence where ambiguity exists—and authority where accountability belongs.`
   - Decision model: Rules / Conventional Software → AI Assistance → Agentic Workflow → Qualified Human Review / Approval.
   - Guardrails:
     - AI output ≠ verified fact
     - AI evidence ≠ verified credential
     - Tool execution ≠ verified outcome
     - Recommendation ≠ authorization
     - AI assistance ≠ clinical judgment

5. **Built by Aloden · Medlivo — Product Proof**
   - Headline: `Healthcare intelligence designed around the full operating workflow.`
   - Medlivo is shown as a larger connected healthcare platform across Workforce, Care, and Operations.
   - Lifecycle: `Need / Referral → Intelligence → Match → Clinician Passport → Credentialing & Readiness → Scheduling / Assignment → Visit / Shift / Service → Documentation / EVV / Time → Billing / Payroll / Operations`.
   - Clinician Passport retained as durable reusable context.
   - Distinct Facility / Workforce and Home Health / Care paths retained.
   - AI surfaces include intake, clinician intelligence, matching/ranking, credentialing, scheduling/capacity, document intelligence, exception/next-best action, and conversational/agentic operations.

6. **Where Healthcare AI Creates Value**
   - Headline: `Measure the healthcare outcome—not the amount of AI in the product.`
   - No fabricated ROI or unsupported performance percentages.
   - Outcome areas: intake/triage, matching/workforce access, credentialing/readiness, scheduling/capacity, documentation/evidence, exception detection, financial/workforce operations, and experience for people.
   - KPI principle: measure whether the healthcare workflow became faster, clearer, safer, or more reliable.

7. **Final CTA**
   - Eyebrow: `BUILD HEALTHCARE AI`
   - Headline: `Turn healthcare complexity into an intelligent product system people can trust.`
   - Capability line: AI Product Engineering · Healthcare Workflows · Agentic Systems · Voice AI · Integration · Modernization.
   - One primary CTA: `Start a Project →`.
   - Closing: `Healthcare AI should not sit beside the workflow. It should make the workflow more intelligent, connected, measurable, and dependable.`
